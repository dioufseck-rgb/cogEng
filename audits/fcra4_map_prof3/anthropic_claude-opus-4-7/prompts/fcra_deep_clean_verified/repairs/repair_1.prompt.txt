CASE NARRATIVE
==============
The consumer disputed a 30-day late notation on an auto loan. The CRA file shows the dispute was received directly on March 1, the account and month were identified, and the consumer supplied a servicer payment ledger. The CRA sent the dispute and document image to the furnisher on the third business day, completed the reinvestigation in 24 calendar days, received a furnisher response verifying the late payment, reviewed the consumer ledger against the furnisher payment history, recorded the item as verified, and mailed results with the revised report 4 business days after completion.
