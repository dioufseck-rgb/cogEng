CASE NARRATIVE
==============
A consumer disputed the date of first delinquency on a credit-card tradeline directly with the CRA. The initial dispute identified the account, disputed field, and basis. On day 18, the consumer submitted a new payoff letter that was relevant to the same disputed field, extending the reinvestigation deadline to 45 days. The CRA completed the reinvestigation on day 43 and sent complete written results 4 business days later. The CRA sent the original dispute to the furnisher on the third business day, but the later payoff letter was not sent or made available to the furnisher. The CRA reviewed the later letter internally, found the reported delinquency date incomplete, modified the tradeline, recorded current status, and the furnisher also modified the item and reported the correction to other CRAs.
